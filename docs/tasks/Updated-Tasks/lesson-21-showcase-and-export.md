# :material-presentation-play: Lesson 21 - Showcase and Export Preparation

Source videos: Whole playlist review

## Learning Intention

Prepare the finished Godot project for sharing, feedback and future improvement.

## Success Criteria

- I can complete a final playtest.
- I can explain how my game works.
- I can collect feedback from another player.
- I can identify export or sharing steps.

## Keywords

| Keyword | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Showcase | Presenting finished work to others. |
| Feedback | Comments that help improve a project. |
| Export | Build a playable version outside the editor. |
| Version | A saved stage of a project. |


## Final Checklist

1. The player can move.
2. The Player can fly in four directions and stays on screen.
3. Sparks can be collected.
4. Score or progress updates.
5. The win screen appears.
6. Restart works.
7. The level is fair to complete.

## Showcase Task

Write a short paragraph explaining:

- The aim of your game.
- The controls.
- One technical feature you are proud of.
- One improvement you would make next.

## Optional Export

Your teacher should test exporting on the school computers before this task.

1. Click **Project > Export**.
2. Click **Add** and choose the platform your teacher specifies.
3. If Godot says export templates are missing, stop and ask your teacher. Do not install software without permission.
4. Choose an export location outside the Godot project folder.
5. Click **Export Project**.
6. Open the exported game and complete one final playtest.

If exporting is unavailable on the school computers, submit the complete Godot project folder instead.
